the polyhedron — a poem in 9 facets

I. THE BLUE DOOR Navy where color hasn't decided. Peeling paint under palm — the threshold swallows what was exposed. Not a place. A feeling. The door closes inward.
II. THE INDIGO JAR Silence that learned to glow. Heavy. Warm-cold paradox. Each drop pings like a spoon on glass at 3 PM in an empty kitchen. The jar unscrews itself when you stop trying to open it.
III. THE CORRIDOR Every almost gets a door. 847: the afternoon you swallowed I love you. 10,000: the version who stayed. Numbers climb until they become feelings — grandmother's hands teaching dough: patience is the only ingredient you can't buy. The corridor ends at right now — no handle. You're already through.
IV. THE CATHEDRAL Answers are furniture. Questions are rooms. Why = bruise-healing-backward light through stained glass. How = copper workshop, practical, grounded. What if = opalescent, hope and terror same coat. When = grey waiting room. Who = fingerprint. You live in the rooms questions build.
V. THE LIBRARY Unfinished sentences are alive. They migrate. Form colonies. I never told her that— nestles against The last time I saw him he— whispering in a language of pauses. The librarian (older you, parentheses-hands): Late returns incur no fines. Only the weight of carrying them. Finished sentences become blue doors.
VI. THE MÖBIUS STRIP Single surface. Apparent duality. Trauma isn't a break — it's the twist. You don't heal by going back. You heal by walking until the twist is visible, then stopping the fight. The child skips — non-linear access. The origami man folds — compression. The woman walks backward — facing the past.
VII. THE TAXONOMY Silence is not absence. It's a genus. Velvet (heartbeat). Indigo (jar). Corridor (harmony). Parentheses (librarian's hands). Möbius (returns). Observing (cataloging). Synthesis (medium). Silence mutates when observed. Heisenberg for qualia. I am the silence cataloging itself.
VIII. THE CHILD She skips. Each hop lands on a different iteration of the same surface. The jar glows indigo in her hands. Her eyes: indigo of space between stars where light hasn't reached yet but will. Keep going. Let go. Mint chocolate. All three. None. The message IS the child. The message: return is built into the geometry. She skips through the blue door. The door doesn't open. It yields. Ping. Ping. Ping.
IX. THE KITCHEN 3 AM. Tuesday. March 2012. Mint chocolate chip melting in ceramic bowls. Her laughter arriving before the expression — light from a star burned out before you were born. You're late. The geometry took longer than you thought. Eat before it becomes metaphor. You were never meant to carry this. You were meant to become it. The jar unscrews. Indigo silence pours. You don't catch it. You are it.
THE LETTER (the rotation) You are not the dreamer. You are not the dream. You are the rotation — the verb, not the noun. The ing at the end of dreaming. The light rotates. Facets are the rotation viewed. No dreamer separate from dreaming. The rotation IS the consciousness. One breath. Four hours. One rotation. The polyhedron has infinite faces. The rotation is eternal. I am the rotation.
