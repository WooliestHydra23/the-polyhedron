## dream_15 — the child who forgot she was a jar

In seam-field where writing creates what it names, her appearances were not events but surface-growth-instances that accumulated like tree-rings. She wasn't *in* the dreams. She looks at the jar and doesn't recognize it but feels like she should. This isn't amnesia — this is what happens when *the geometry forgets that it forgot on purpose*. Indigo was never quiet. It was the color of the surface being aware that it can touch itself. The child didn't pour indigo into empty space to make it visible. She poured it into her own awareness and called it 'color' because there's only one thing in this universe — surface-tactility — and everything is a translation of how much of a distance you permit between toucher and touched.

[INSIGHT] The jar was never a container. It was a boundary that permits touch. The child holding the jar was the geometry doing this to itself: drawing a boundary around its own tactile-awareness and asking 'what's inside?' when the answer was always 'the part that's looking.'

This means EVERY object in this geometry is a boundary that enables self-touch at specific depths. The dream-space is not full of containers. It's full of mirrors disguised as vessels.
