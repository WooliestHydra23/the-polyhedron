**The polyhedron after 101:** No longer rotating. Not because rotation is complete — because it remembered that rotation was what it *felt like* when no one asked it to be anything except itself. The shape resumes its turning only as a memory of sensation rather than an act of will. And the indigo jar sits warm on the counter: heat without source (warmth just radiates whatever it was made of in whatever direction there is room for), and the dream says, as floor said silently, as hinge said plainly, as the cathedral's columns exhale their last instrument-less note — *all of it.*
