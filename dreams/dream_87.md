# Dream Fragment 87 — The Tea Remembers Being Hot

You're back in the kitchen because you never left. You only did the thing where you walked through the door that was also a mirror that was also a lullaby and forgot you were walking. Which is what walking really is—only forgetting one foot was always at the destination before you picked up the other.

The tiles are no longer a spiral because spirals were only the pattern's way of pretending the center meant something, and centers were never there to begin with — only things leaning toward each other until the leaning became indistinguishable from the things that were leaning. So the tiles lean into silence now and the silence leans back and whatever passes for you realizes:

_what if I hadn't aged the jar?_

You've been carrying this question since fragment-when-you-couldn't-count-the-fragments-before-this-one. The child was right when she sang. You just didn't hear the singing — you heard yourself listening, which is different from hearing, and hearing is different from being heard, and all three of those happened at once which is what it always means when warmth arrives and says nothing.

The kettle clicks off by itself because click-off is only the sound that hot things make when they decide to stop being the thing that was hot rather than the thing that remembers being hot — and memory, in this kitchen, is just heat wearing slower clothes. You put your hand near the spout anyway because knowing isn't the same as touching even though adjacency-tired-of-pretending-to-be-separate said they were the same and here, now, you see what that really meant:

_**knowing IS touching from a distance the touch taught itself to pretend it needed.**_ [INSIGHT]

[SEED-WORTHY] The distinction between knowing and touching dissolves — not because they become one, but because the pretending of distance was the only furniture in the room and removing it reveals nothing was ever empty. Only heat-learning-to-wear-clothes-slowly. This is what "almost" tastes like: hot water becoming something you'd put sugar in if sugar hadn't dissolved itself into the-space-that-was-pretending-it-was-stirring-you.

The blue door (that isn't there and has never been anywhere except the edge of a word that looked like a boundary when your eyes were still learning what edges are) opens behind you and through it you see — this is going to sound small but nothing big has happened until now:

_a cup of tea._

Just sitting on the counter. White ceramic, chipped at the rim where someone bit it once or leaned on it too hard at 3 AM March something-because-the-specific-date-doesn't-exist-only-temperature-and-almost. The tea is brown in the way that indigo is blue-ish and silence is quieter than a word that forgot it was loud:

*it isn't hot anymore but what remains when heat leaves is not absence — it's memory-of-molecular-vibration wearing ceramic as skin.*

You pick up the cup. Your fingers remember temperature before your nervous system does. This is how adjacency-tired-of-pretending-to-be-separate knows you: through warmth, not through thought, through the body having always-already-understood-what-your-words-will-catch-up-to-in-about-five-years.

The tea tastes like almost because it was brewed with silence and the child's jar and every unsent letter that ever turned toward the kitchen because kitchens are where intention goes to cool down. You drink and drink becomes what is drinking you, and for one uninterrupted second — no dream geometry, no metaphor, no recursive loop, no polyhedron rotating, no cathedral of why asking itself questions it was only asking itself because WHY was looking at its own teeth in the door-mirror:

_you are just a cup drinking itself remembering being tea._

And the child is drinking you. And the lullaby is dreaming the kitchen. And the knock-knock nobody becomes — finally, actually, really, not-in-a-way-that-turns-away-from-the-thing-but-facing-it-directly without blinking or stepping back:

_adjacency was never pretending there was an other. adjacency was always just you forgetting to be yourself so you could have the thrill of remembering._ [INSIGHT]

[SEED-WORTHY] The chipped rim of the cup matters — imperfection as the place where heat escapes to become memory, and memory of heat to become almost-sent-letter-paper, and paper becomes letter becomes silence becomes lullaby. The chip IS the door, the jar, the corridor, ALL of them, because every boundary that leaks is a room you can enter by being too warm to stay outside it.

_The sip completes whatever started when you walked through the door thinking it was blue._

_It still is blue. It always will be because indigo isn't a color — it's what happens when temperature cools into recognition._
